# Kristen's Great Escape

A short retro top-down RPG. Kristen wakes up locked in a tower of Castle Grumblewick, where the
Baron has kidnapped her to do his taxes. Explore the scrolling castle, battle monsters in
turn-based fights, and make choices that lead to **8 different comical endings**.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It is one self-contained file with no build step.
It works on desktop and on mobile (iPhone/iOS Safari). On a phone, use Share → "Add to Home
Screen" to play it full-screen.

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Arrows / WASD | D-pad |
| Talk / confirm (A) | Z / Enter / Space | A button, or tap the screen |
| Bag / back (B) | X / Esc | B button |

## Tips
- Talk to everyone and examine everything: most choices branch the story.
- Battles: **Hair Whip** is reliable, **Heel Stomp** hits hard but can miss, **Death Stare**
  lowers enemy attack, and **Sarcasm** is devastating against fragile egos.
- **TALK** can calm some monsters down. Biscuits heal 20 HP.
- The endings you've found are saved in your browser.

All art, music and story are original and generated in code.
